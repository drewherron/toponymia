# Content license

Toponymia separates two things, licensed differently:

- **The software** (everything in `server/` and `web/`) is licensed under the
  **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3** — see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
- **The wiki content** — articles, etymology text, structured name and
  derivation fields, and talk-page posts contributed through the site — is
  licensed under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
  International License (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>

Keeping the two separate lets the content be reused on its own terms — text
can flow in and out under CC BY-SA, subject to the license terms — independent
of how the software is licensed.

## Attribution

Reuse of Toponymia content under CC BY-SA 4.0 requires attribution to the
authors. Attribution is satisfied by crediting
"Toponymia contributors" with a link back to the specific article and its
revision history — the authoritative list of contributors.

## Contributing

By saving an edit or posting to a talk page, contributors agree to release
their contribution under CC BY-SA 4.0 on the terms described in
[`TERMS.md`](TERMS.md). Contributors retain copyright in their work; the
license is what lets the wiki host and redistribute it.

## Upstream data

Toponymia also builds on open data from other projects, under their own terms:

- **OpenStreetMap** — map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under
  the **Open Database License (ODbL)**. <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>
- **OpenFreeMap** — hosted vector basemap tiles. <https://openfreemap.org/>
- **Wikidata** — identifiers and multilingual labels, released under **CC0**
  (public domain). <https://www.wikidata.org/>
