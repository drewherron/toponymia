Overrides allauth's own email_confirmation_message.txt.

**This is the change-email mail, not the signup one.**
`AccountAdapter.send_confirmation_mail` branches on its `signup` flag and only
falls here when the flag is false; the signup body lives in
`email_confirmation_signup_message.txt`. Stock allauth points both names at
this one file, and the two were shared here too until the borrowed signup
wording was noticed arriving on a change ("Someone used this email address to
create an account", when no account was created and none is at risk). See that
file's comment for why the split is the fix rather than a hedged middle text.

With ACCOUNT_CHANGE_EMAIL the new address *replaces* the old, and there is no
add-a-second-address affordance anywhere in the UI — the only thing that
reaches this template is the "Change email" panel in AccountDialog.tsx. So the
mail can name the actual situation instead of describing address confirmation
in general. **If a second-address feature ever lands, this text stops being
true** and the two cases need splitting again.

The reassurance is the mirror of the signup one, and it is different in
substance rather than in phrasing: on signup, "ignore this" is safe because the
account cannot be used until it is confirmed. Here it is safe because *nothing
is stored yet* — allauth holds the new address nowhere until the code comes
back, so an ignored mail leaves the account exactly as it was, still on its
current address. That is the sentence a worried reader needs, and it is the one
the signup wording cannot carry.

No username, for the same reason as the signup mail: the reader who did this is
looking at the browser tab that just asked them for the code, and the reader who
did not is looking at a name that tells them nothing actionable.

New msgids with no catalog behind them, deliberately: L10N.md settles that
allauth mails stay English. The blocktranslate tags are kept so the strings stay
extractable if that ever changes, and the `code`/`activate_url` branch is kept
so that flipping ACCOUNT_EMAIL_VERIFICATION_BY_CODE_ENABLED stays a setting
change rather than a silently broken email.
{% endcomment %}
